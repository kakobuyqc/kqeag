Litbuy shopping guide

Save the link.
Check the details.
Then decide.

A spreadsheet becomes useful when it helps you compare like with like. Keep observations separate from seller claims, and leave uncertain details marked as unverified.

These steps apply to a small personal shortlist as well as a larger collection of fashion product links.

  1. 1

    Choose a specific category

    Start with what you need: daily shoes, a warm layer, a matching set or an accessory. A focused category makes comparisons easier than a mixed list of unrelated items.

  2. 2

    Record the destination listing

    Save Litbuy product links alongside the item name, seller and date checked. Reopen the destination before ordering because a saved price or screenshot can become outdated.

  3. 3

    Compare measurements and evidence

    Use the size chart, material description and available product images. Compare measurements with an item you own, and ask the seller about details the listing does not explain.

  4. 4

    Review the total before paying

    Separate the product price from delivery, service fees and applicable import charges. Confirm current availability, return terms and the final checkout amount.

A practical comparison framework

What belongs in your shortlist?

Use this structure for a Litbuy shoes spreadsheet, a clothing list or a mixed accessories collection.

Suggested spreadsheet columns and verification notes
Field What to record What to check
Product and category A descriptive name, category and destination link. The link still opens the intended item and variant.
Price and currency The displayed item price, currency and date checked. Whether the selected size or color changes the price.
Size and fit Garment measurements or footwear sizing details. Units, measurement method and stated tolerance.
Materials The seller's fabric, upper or accessory material description. Missing composition details and any unsupported claims.
Images and condition Relevant listing images and notes about visible details. Whether photos show the selected variant and actual condition.
Delivery and fees Available delivery estimates and separate cost components. Which charges are included and which remain estimates.
Seller and returns Seller identity, contact options and published return terms. Deadlines, exclusions and responsibility for return shipping.

This is a comparison template, not a live inventory feed. Verify all prices, stock information and purchase terms on the destination website.

Understand clothing fit

For hoodie finds and T-shirt finds, use garment measurements rather than size letters alone. A clothing size guide and clear fabric composition help explain how an item may fit and feel.

Plan a complete outfit

Compare jacket layering with pants measurements before building an outfit. For matching sets, check each piece separately; review jersey sizing and underwear fabric for their specific uses.

Headwear sizing and adjustable cap fit deserve a place in your notes. For accessories, compare jewelry dimensions, accessory materials and the available product image review before deciding.

Start with an item price comparison, then add a shipping cost estimate and any shopping service fees. A return policy check and a clear total purchase cost make the final comparison more useful.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping list used to organize product links and comparison notes. Useful columns include category, seller, listed price, size chart, material, image references and the date a listing was checked. This page explains that workflow and links to CAFANS categories; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet.

How do I use Litbuy product links?

Open the destination listing, confirm that it matches the item you intended to view, and check its available sizes, materials, current price and seller information. A saved link alone does not confirm stock, authenticity or compatibility with a particular shopping service.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check whether the measurements describe the body or the garment, note the units, and allow for any measurement tolerance stated by the seller.

Does a spreadsheet price include international delivery?

Do not assume it does. Separate the item price from domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable import charges. Use the destination checkout or shipping estimate to verify the total before paying.
